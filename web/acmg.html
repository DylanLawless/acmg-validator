ACMG criteria

These are the 28 ACMG/AMP evidence criteria recognised by this toolkit. Each code describes a type of evidence that may support a pathogenic or benign interpretation. The table explains the criterion, its default strength and whether additional notes or caveat checks are required.

The definitions come from the versioned ACMG catalogue (JSON) used by the validator. They are a reference for the implementation, not a substitute for current ClinGen or gene-specific expert-panel guidance. The default strength may need adjustment under the guideline specification you are using. On a narrow screen, scroll the tables sideways to read all columns.

Pathogenic evidence criteria

The PVS, PS, PM and PP groups describe evidence that may support pathogenicity. An accepted criterion requires an appropriate evidence review; it does not automatically establish a QEM result.

Recording results

Use criterion for the code, status for the ACMG review decision and evidence_status for the underlying evidence. Use source_ref and verified to document that ACMG evidence. A separate qem_raw_outcome records whether the negatively worded QEM invalidation check was confirmed, ruled out with verified qualifying evidence, or could not be evaluated.
